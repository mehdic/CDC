- Browse available deliveries in your area

- Turn-by-turn navigation to each delivery address

## Tips for Success

### Maximize Your Earnings

1. **Accept High-Priority Deliveries**
   - Urgent deliveries typically pay more
   - Complete them on time for bonuses

2. **Monitor Active Bonuses**
   - Check Earnings dashboard daily
   - Work toward bonus targets
   - Bonuses add significant earnings

3. **Maintain On-Time Rate**
   - Plan efficient routes
   - Leave early for tight time windows
   - On-time bonuses increase earnings

4. **Complete Special Handlings Correctly**
   - Never skip acknowledgments
   - Follow all requirements exactly
   - Proper execution builds reliability rating

### Best Practices

1. **Before Each Shift**
   - Charge your device fully
   - Check for app updates
   - Ensure GPS is enabled
   - Review active bonuses

2. **During Deliveries**
   - Keep app open during navigation
   - Don't force close the app
   - Submit proof immediately after delivery
   - Keep device charged (use power bank for long shifts)

3. **After Each Delivery**
   - Submit proof of delivery immediately
   - Check next delivery details
   - Monitor your location tracking
   - Review earnings updates

4. **Maintenance**
   - Restart device weekly
   - Update app when available
   - Clear cache monthly
   - Keep storage above 200 MB free
