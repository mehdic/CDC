## App Installation

### For iOS Users

1. **Download from App Store**
   - Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad
   - Search for "MetaPharm Delivery"
   - Tap "Get" to download the app
   - Use Face ID, Touch ID, or Apple ID to confirm the download

2. **Minimum Requirements**
   - iOS 14.0 or later
   - At least 150 MB of free storage space
   - Active internet connection (WiFi or cellular)

3. **After Installation**
   - The app will appear on your home screen
   - Tap the MetaPharm Delivery icon to launch the app
   - Wait for initial setup to complete (first launch may take 30-60 seconds)

### For Android Users

1. **Download from Google Play**
   - Open Google Play Store on your Android device
   - Search for "MetaPharm Delivery"
   - Tap "Install"
   - Confirm that you accept the app permissions

2. **Minimum Requirements**
   - Android 10.0 or later
   - At least 150 MB of free storage space
   - Active internet connection (WiFi or cellular)

3. **After Installation**
   - The app will appear in your app drawer
   - Tap the MetaPharm Delivery icon to launch the app
   - Wait for initial setup to complete (first launch may take 30-60 seconds)

### Updating the App

- **iOS**: Open App Store > Tap your profile icon > Scroll to "MetaPharm Delivery" > Tap "Update"
- **Android**: Open Google Play Store > Search "MetaPharm Delivery" > Tap "Update"

## Account Setup & Login

### Creating Your Account (First Time)

If you're a new delivery personnel, contact your pharmacy manager to:
- Register your account
- Receive your email address and temporary password
- Verify your contact information

### Logging In

1. **Open the App**
   - Launch MetaPharm Delivery on your device
   - You'll see the login screen with the MetaPharm branding

2. **Enter Credentials**
   - Email: Enter your assigned delivery email (e.g., `delivery@metapharm.ch`)
   - Password: Enter your password
   - Tap the **Login** button

3. **First Login Process**
   - System will verify your credentials
   - You may be asked to confirm your location
   - Grant necessary permissions (covered in [App Permissions](#app-permissions) section)

4. **HIN e-ID Login (Swiss Healthcare Provider)**
   - Alternative secure login method
   - Tap **"Login with HIN e-ID"**
   - Follow the authentication flow with your Swiss healthcare e-ID provider
   - This provides enhanced security for healthcare data access

### Forgotten Password

If you forget your password:
1. Tap **"Forgot Password?"** on the login screen
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for password reset link
4. Follow the instructions to create a new password
5. Return to the app and log in with your new password

### Account Security Tips

- **Never share your login credentials** with other delivery personnel
- **Keep your password secure** and change it regularly
- **Log out** when finished with deliveries, especially on shared devices
- **Enable HIN e-ID** if available for your account - it's more secure than passwords

## First Delivery Walkthrough

### Step 1: View Available Deliveries

After logging in, you'll see the **Delivery List** screen:

1. **Available Deliveries Tab** (default view)
   - Shows all unclaimed deliveries in your area
   - Each delivery card shows:
     - Patient name
     - Delivery address
     - Distance from your current location
     - Estimated delivery time
     - Priority level (Urgent, High, Medium, Low)
     - Any special handling alerts

2. **My Deliveries Tab**
   - Shows deliveries you've accepted
   - Lists your active and pending deliveries
   - Helps you track your current workload

3. **Search & Filter**
   - Search by patient name or city name
   - Use filter tabs to switch between available and your deliveries
   - Pull down to refresh the list for latest deliveries

### Step 2: Accept a Delivery

1. **Select a Delivery**
   - Tap on any delivery card in the "Available" list
   - Review the delivery details screen

2. **Review Delivery Information**
   - Patient name and address
   - Phone number (if available for contact)
   - Special instructions or notes
   - Estimated payment for completing delivery
   - Special handling requirements (if any)

3. **Accept the Delivery**
   - Tap the **"Accept Delivery"** button
   - The app will confirm acceptance
   - The delivery moves to your "My Deliveries" tab

### Step 3: Navigate to Pickup Location

Once you've accepted a delivery:

1. **Go to Pickup**
   - From your delivery details, tap **"Go to Pickup"**
   - You'll see pharmacy address and navigation options
   - Tap **"Open Maps"** to use your device's GPS navigation
   - Follow turn-by-turn directions to the pharmacy

2. **Arrive at Pharmacy**
   - When you arrive, the app will recognize your location
   - Tap **"I've Arrived"** on the delivery screen

### Step 4: Acknowledge Special Handling (If Required)

Some deliveries have special handling requirements:

1. **View Requirements Screen**
   - Before pickup, review all special handling alerts
   - Examples: Cold chain maintenance, ID verification, controlled substances

2. **Understand Each Requirement**
   - Read the detailed description for each alert
   - Review the list of specific requirements
   - Ask pharmacy staff if you have questions

3. **Acknowledge Each Requirement**
   - Check the checkbox for each requirement you understand
   - Your acknowledgment is timestamped for compliance
   - Tap **"Proceed to Pickup"** only when all are acknowledged

### Step 5: Prepare for Delivery

1. **At the Pharmacy**
   - Provide your delivery ID to pharmacy staff
   - Collect the packages for this delivery
   - Verify package count matches delivery details
   - Check for any special handling instructions on the packages

2. **QR Code Scanning (Optional)**
   - Some pharmacies may ask you to scan QR codes on packages
   - This verifies you have the correct packages
   - Scanning is optional but recommended for accuracy

### Step 6: Navigate to Delivery Address

1. **Start Navigation**
   - Open your "My Deliveries" tab
   - Select the delivery
   - Tap **"Navigate to Delivery"**
   - Review the delivery address and any special instructions

2. **Real-Time Tracking**
   - The app shows your location on the map
   - Distance and estimated arrival time are displayed
   - Navigation info updates as you drive

3. **Special Handling Alerts**
   - The app displays any special requirements during navigation
   - Examples: "Cold Chain Required - Keep in Insulated Bag"
   - "Controlled Substance - ID Verification Required"

### Step 7: Capture Proof of Delivery

When you arrive at the delivery address:

1. **Prepare Proof of Delivery Screen**
   - Tap **"Complete Delivery"**
   - Review the required fields (marked with *)

2. **Fill Required Information**
   - **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the delivery
   - **Delivery Photo**: Take a photo of the delivered packages or patient
   - **GPS Location**: Confirm your GPS location is enabled

3. **Optional Information**
   - **Signature**: Ask recipient to sign (on-screen signature capture)
   - **ID Photo**: Photograph recipient's ID if required
   - **Notes**: Add any delivery notes or special circumstances

4. **Submit Proof**
   - Review all information is complete
   - Tap **"Complete Delivery"** button
   - The app will upload the proof to the pharmacy

5. **Confirmation**
   - You'll see a success message
   - The delivery is marked as complete
   - You can now accept another delivery

## App Permissions

The MetaPharm Delivery App requires certain permissions to function properly. Here's what each permission does:

### Location Services (Required)

**Why needed**: To track your GPS location for navigation and delivery confirmation

- **Permission name**: "Location"
- **When requested**: On first app launch
- **What to select**: "Allow While Using App" or "Always Allow"
- **Recommendation**: Select "Always Allow" for best experience during multi-stop routes

### Camera (Required for QR & Photos)

**Why needed**: To scan QR codes on packages and take delivery proof photos

- **Permission name**: "Camera"
- **When requested**: When you first use QR scanner or photo features
- **What to select**: "Allow"
- **Note**: You can manage this in Settings > MetaPharm Delivery > Camera

### Photos/Media Library (Optional)

**Why needed**: To upload photos for proof of delivery

- **Permission name**: "Photos" or "Media Library"
- **When requested**: When uploading delivery photos
- **What to select**: "Allow"
- **Note**: You can select which photos the app accesses

### Contacts (Optional)

**Why needed**: To quickly contact pharmacy or patient if needed

- **Permission name**: "Contacts"
- **When requested**: When you try to call/message contacts
- **What to select**: "Allow"
- **Note**: You can manage this in Settings

### Microphone (Optional)

**Why needed**: For voice note features or video call support

- **Permission name**: "Microphone"
- **When requested**: When accessing voice features
- **What to select**: "Allow"
- **Note**: Only needed if you use voice features

### Managing Permissions After Installation

**iOS:**
1. Go to Settings > MetaPharm Delivery
2. Enable or disable each permission
3. Return to app to apply changes

**Android:**
1. Go to Settings > Apps > MetaPharm Delivery
2. Tap "Permissions"
3. Enable or disable each permission
4. Return to app to apply changes
