## Account & Authentication

### Q: How do I reset my password?

**A:** To reset your password:
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

See [Getting Started Guide](01-GETTING_STARTED.md) for detailed instructions.

### Q: Can I use multiple devices with my account?

**A:** You can log in on multiple devices, but:
- Only one device can be actively logged in at a time
- Logging in on a new device logs you out from the previous device
- For security, use your personal device when possible

### Q: What does HIN e-ID authentication mean?

**A:** HIN e-ID is a Swiss healthcare authentication system:
- More secure than username/password
- Uses your Swiss healthcare professional e-ID
- Complies with Swiss healthcare regulations
- Optional - you can use email/password instead
- Better for sensitive healthcare data access

See [Getting Started Guide](01-GETTING_STARTED.md#hin-eid-login-swiss-healthcare-provider) for setup.

### Q: What should I do if someone knows my password?

**A:** If you think your password is compromised:
1. Immediately change your password using "Forgot Password?"
2. Contact your pharmacy manager
3. Request account security review
4. Never share password with anyone

### Q: How long is my login session?

**A:** Login sessions typically last:
- **Inactive timeout**: 8-24 hours without activity
- **Auto-logout**: When you force close the app
- **Device restart**: May require re-login
- **Expiration**: For security reasons, all sessions expire

Just log in again - it only takes a moment.

### Q: Can I have a different username?

**A:** Your username is your email address:
- Set by pharmacy administrator
- Cannot self-change
- Contact your pharmacy manager to modify
- Usually format: firstname.lastname@metapharm.ch

## Deliveries & Payments

### Q: How do I accept a delivery?

**A:** To accept a delivery:
1. View the Delivery List
2. Tap on any delivery card in the "Available" section
3. Review the delivery details
4. Tap "Accept Delivery"
5. It moves to your "My Deliveries" tab

See [Getting Started Guide](01-GETTING_STARTED.md#first-delivery-walkthrough) for full walkthrough.

### Q: What's the difference between "Available" and "My Deliveries"?

**A:**
- **Available**: Unclaimed deliveries anyone can accept
- **My Deliveries**: Deliveries you've accepted and are responsible for

Use the filter tabs to switch between them.

### Q: How much do I earn per delivery?

**A:** Payment varies based on:
- **Base payment**: $3-5 per delivery (typical)
- **Distance**: Further deliveries pay more
- **Priority**: Urgent deliveries may pay premium
- **Bonuses**: Incentive bonuses add to earnings
- **Demand**: Peak times may offer higher rates

Check the estimated payment on each delivery card before accepting.

### Q: When do I get paid?

**A:** Payment processing timeline:
- **Completion**: Immediately after submitting proof
- **Processing**: Weekly payment runs (usually Friday)
- **Transfer**: 1-3 business days to your bank account
- **Viewing**: Updated on Earnings dashboard same day

Payments are made weekly by direct deposit to your registered bank account.

### Q: Can I decline or cancel a delivery?

**A:** Cancellation policy:
- **Before leaving**: You can decline available deliveries (no penalty)
- **After accepting**: You can cancel if unreasonable circumstances
- **Penalty**: Multiple cancellations may affect your rating
- **Contact pharmacy**: If you must cancel, call pharmacy immediately

Canceling without good reason may affect future delivery availability.

### Q: What if I can't find the delivery address?

**A:** If the address is hard to find:
1. Use the in-app navigation to get close
2. Check address on delivery details
3. Call or message the patient if number available
4. Ask pharmacy staff for clarification
5. If truly unreachable, contact pharmacy

Never guess or deliver to wrong address. Contact pharmacy for correct info.

### Q: What if the patient isn't home?

**A:** If customer is not available:
1. Try knocking/ringing bell again
2. Wait reasonable time (5-10 minutes)
3. Check delivery instructions for special locations (mailbox, etc.)
4. Call patient if number available
5. Call pharmacy for guidance

**Don't leave medication unattended** unless explicitly authorized. Contact pharmacy for instructions.

### Q: Can I work fewer hours?

**A:** You control your schedule:
- Accept as many or few deliveries as you want
- Work full-time, part-time, or gig-based
- No minimum hours required
- Earnings depend on deliveries completed
- Peak hours (lunch, evening) have more available deliveries

It's flexible - you decide when to work.

### Q: How do bonuses work?

**A:** Bonuses are incentive rewards:
- **Types**: Sprint, distance, rating, time-sensitive
- **Tracking**: View on Earnings dashboard
- **Progress**: Real-time updates as you complete deliveries
- **Reward**: Automatically paid when you hit target
- **Expiration**: Each bonus has deadline

Monitor your Earnings dashboard to see active bonuses and work toward them.

See [Feature Guides - Earnings Dashboard](02-FEATURE_GUIDES.md#bonus-opportunities) for details.

### Q: My earnings disappeared. What happened?

**A:** Earnings not showing could mean:
- **Different period selected**: Switch between Today/Week/Month/Year
- **Offline**: Earnings require internet connection
- **Not synced**: Wait 2-3 minutes after completion
- **Pending completion**: Deliveries must show as "Delivered"
- **Failed delivery**: Incomplete deliveries don't pay

Refresh the screen or restart the app and check again.

## App Features

### Q: What's the difference between QR scanning and proof of delivery?

**A:**
- **QR Scanning**: Optional package verification (confirms correct packages)
- **Proof of Delivery**: Required documentation (photo, recipient name, GPS, signature)

Both help ensure accurate deliveries but proof of delivery is mandatory to complete.

### Q: Is proof of delivery required for every delivery?

**A:** Yes, proof of delivery is required:
- Must take delivery photo
- Must enter recipient name
- Must have GPS location
- Signature optional (unless required by special handling)
- Photo with ID optional (unless required)

Cannot mark delivery complete without these.

### Q: What happens to my photos after I submit?

**A:** Photos are:
- **Encrypted**: Protected in transit and storage
- **Stored**: Kept with delivery records
- **Private**: Only visible to authorized pharmacy staff
- **Compliant**: GDPR and healthcare privacy protected
- **Deletable**: Old photos may be archived after period

Your privacy is protected - photos are not shared or sold.

### Q: Can I edit a completed delivery?

**A:** Once proof submitted:
- Cannot be edited
- Locked for record-keeping
- If mistake, contact pharmacy manager
- Manager can review and flag if needed
- Future deliveries can be corrected

Proof of delivery is a permanent record for compliance.

### Q: What if I need to return a delivery?

**A:** To return a delivery:
1. **Cannot deliver**: If impossible to deliver for any reason
2. **Contact pharmacy**: Call or message immediately
3. **Return instructions**: Pharmacy provides how to return
4. **Update status**: Mark as "Failed" or "Returned" in app
5. **No payment**: Returned deliveries don't pay

Always contact pharmacy first - don't just return without instruction.

## Navigation & GPS

### Q: Why is navigation showing a different route than I expected?

**A:** The app uses:
- **Road network**: Following actual roads, not straight line
- **Traffic data**: Real-time traffic from mapping service
- **Optimization**: Shortest road-based route (not distance)
- **Restrictions**: One-way streets, no-entry zones

The app picks the best driving route available.

### Q: Can I use my own GPS or maps app?

**A:** You can use other apps:
- In-app navigation is recommended
- Other maps apps work alongside
- The app still tracks your location
- In-app navigation provides delivery context
- You can open Apple Maps or Google Maps from app too

Use whichever works best for you.

### Q: What if GPS is off at delivery location?

**A:** GPS is required for proof of delivery:
- Cannot submit without GPS location
- Must have Location Services enabled
- Move outdoors if signal weak
- Wait 30-60 seconds for lock
- Can submit later when GPS available (if offline)

GPS ensures delivery location is accurately recorded.

### Q: Why does navigation seem to take the long way?

**A:** Several factors affect routing:
- **Road conditions**: Construction, closed roads
- **Traffic**: Real-time traffic data affects route
- **One-ways**: System navigates using legal roads
- **Restrictions**: Truck routes, pedestrian zones
- **Freshness**: Map data updates regularly

The app selects safest, legal route even if not shortest.

## Technical & Device

### Q: What iOS/Android version do I need?

**A:** Minimum requirements:
- **iOS**: 14.0 or later (iPhone/iPad)
- **Android**: 10.0 or later
- Check your device version in Settings > About
- Older devices may not be supported

If your device is too old, you may need a newer device.

### Q: How much storage does the app use?

**A:** Storage requirements:
- **App size**: About 150 MB
- **Cache**: 50-100 MB additional
- **Photos/data**: Varies by deliveries completed
- **Total recommended**: 500 MB free space
- **Keep free**: Maintain 200 MB minimum at all times

Uninstall if storage critical and reinstall later.

### Q: Can I use app on tablet or iPad?

**A:** Tablet compatibility:
- **iPad**: Fully supported (iOS 14+)
- **Android tablets**: Supported (Android 10+)
- **Recommended**: Phone is preferred (easier GPS/navigation)
- **Works**: App functions same on tablet
- **Delivery**: Most delivery people use phones

Both work but phones are more practical for mobile deliveries.

### Q: Does the app drain my battery quickly?

**A:** Battery usage:
- **GPS/Maps**: Heaviest drain (continuous position tracking)
- **Typical shift**: 5-8 hours use per full battery charge
- **WiFi vs cellular**: WiFi uses less battery
- **Brightness**: Reducing brightness saves battery
- **Recommendation**: Carry power bank for long shifts

Turn off unnecessary features (Bluetooth, extra brightness) to conserve.

### Q: How much data does the app use?

**A:** Data consumption:
- **Maps/Navigation**: 10-20 MB per hour (heavy)
- **Proof uploads**: 2-5 MB per delivery (large photos)
- **General use**: 1-2 MB per hour
- **Offline mode**: Minimal data when offline
- **Recommendation**: Unlimited or generous data plan

Use WiFi when available to save cellular data.

### Q: Will the app work if my phone is old/slow?

**A:** The app works on:
- **Minimum OS**: iOS 14.0+ or Android 10.0+
- **Old but supported**: Older supported devices work slower
- **Performance**: May experience lag or crashes on very old devices
- **Workaround**: Close other apps, restart device
- **Upgrade**: If frequent crashes, may need newer device

Newer devices provide better experience but app works on supported older phones.

## Privacy & Security

### Q: Is my location tracked all the time?

**A:** Location tracking:
- **During deliveries**: Yes, tracked for safety and compliance
- **Off-duty**: No tracking when app closed
- **Purpose**: Safety, routing, proof of delivery
- **Protection**: Encrypted in transit, secure storage
- **Control**: You enable/disable location in device settings

Tracking is only active when app is in use.

### Q: Who can see my location?

**A:** Location access:
- **Pharmacy manager**: Can see your active deliveries in real-time
- **Your data**: You can see your own location in app
- **Platform**: Limited to authorized staff
- **Public**: Your location is never shared publicly
- **Privacy**: Protected under GDPR healthcare regulations

Only necessary personnel see your location for operational safety.

### Q: What data does the app collect?

**A:** Data collected includes:
- **Account**: Email, name, contact info
- **Deliveries**: Addresses, times, status
- **Location**: GPS coordinates during deliveries
- **Photos**: Proof of delivery images
- **Usage**: App feature usage analytics
- **Performance**: Error and crash logs

All data is encrypted and protected under Swiss healthcare laws.

### Q: Can I be tracked when app is closed?

**A:** When app is closed:
- **No tracking**: App doesn't track when not running
- **Location services**: Device location may be on (your choice)
- **Background**: App doesn't run in background for tracking
- **Notifications**: You receive notifications but not tracked

You control location via device Settings > Location.

### Q: What about my payment information?

**A:** Payment security:
- **Never stored locally**: On app or device
- **Encrypted**: All transmission encrypted
- **PCI compliant**: Payment info handled securely
- **Bank transfer**: Direct to your bank account
- **Protection**: Subject to bank security standards

Your payment information is as secure as your bank account.

### Q: Is my ID photo private?

**A:** ID photo protection:
- **Encrypted**: Protected with encryption
- **Limited access**: Only authorized pharmacy staff
- **Compliance**: GDPR and healthcare privacy laws
- **Retention**: Kept only as long as needed
- **Not shared**: Never shared with third parties

ID photos are treated as sensitive healthcare data.

### Q: Who has access to my delivery information?

**A:** Delivery data access:
- **Pharmacy**: Manager can view your deliveries
- **Patient**: Patient may see delivery status
- **You**: Can see all your delivery history
- **Compliance**: Only authorized personnel
- **Audit**: Data available for regulatory audits

Access is limited to those who need it operationally.
