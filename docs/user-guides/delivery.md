## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Create Account**
   - Download MetaPharm Connect app
   - Select "Delivery Personnel" role
   - Enter personal information:
     - Full name
     - Phone number
     - Email address
   - Set secure password (12+ characters)

2. **Identity Verification**
   - Provide ID photo (driver's license or passport)
   - Selfie for identity verification
   - Swiss driving license (required)
   - Insurance information
   - Background check authorization
   - Processing typically takes 24-48 hours

3. **Background Check**
   - MetaPharm conducts safety screening
   - Swiss criminal record check (if required by location)
   - You'll be notified when approved
   - Approval email contains your access codes

4. **Vehicle Information** (if using personal vehicle)
   - Vehicle type (car, motorcycle, bike)
   - License plate
   - Insurance details
   - Vehicle photo for verification

5. **Payment Setup**
   - Provide bank account or payment method
   - Direct deposit for earnings
   - Weekly or daily payouts (configurable)
   - Tax information for invoicing

### First Login

1. **Download the App**
   - iOS: App Store
   - Android: Google Play
   - Search for "MetaPharm Delivery"

2. **Login & Preferences**
   - Use email and password from signup
   - Set up two-factor authentication (optional but recommended)
   - Enable location services (required)
   - Allow push notifications (highly recommended)

3. **Availability Settings**
   - Set your working hours
   - Select which days you work
   - Specify geographic area(s)
   - Save preferences

4. **First Delivery**
   - System may start with limited area
   - After few successful deliveries, area expands
   - You can request area expansion in Settings

## Accepting Deliveries

### Finding & Accepting Deliveries

**1. View Available Deliveries**
- Open Home screen
- Scroll through available deliveries
- Click on delivery for full details

**2. Delivery Details**
- From address (pharmacy)
- To address (patient home)
- Distance from your location
- Estimated delivery time
- What's being delivered (general category)
- Estimated earnings
- Your rating (for transparency)
- Special handling requirements (if any)

**3. Accept Delivery**
- Click "Accept Delivery" button
- You're now responsible for this delivery
- Delivery appears in your deliveries list
- Pharmacy is notified
- You can see pickup address

**4. Navigation to Pharmacy**
- Click "Navigate to Pharmacy"
- Opens your preferred maps app
- Shows real-time route
- Updated ETA

### Delivery Acceptance Rules

**Availability**
- You can only accept deliveries when "Online"
- Turn "Online" in Home screen to accept deliveries
- Turn "Offline" when not available
- No deliveries assigned while offline

**Geographic Limits**
- Only accept deliveries in your approved area
- System prevents accepting outside zone
- Request area expansion in Settings
- Expansion reviewed after 20 successful deliveries

**Cancellation Policy**
- You can cancel accepted delivery before pickup
- After pickup, you must complete delivery
- Multiple cancellations may reduce deliveries offered
- Emergency cancellations supported (explain in app)

**Special Requests**
- Some patients request specific delivery times
- Accept time-specific deliveries carefully
- Late arrival affects your rating
- Set realistic ETA when accepting

### Delivery Earnings

**Base Fare Calculation**
- Distance from pharmacy to delivery address
- Typical: $2-8 per delivery
- Longer distances pay more
- Peak hours may offer surge pricing (1.5x)

**Bonuses & Incentives**
- High ratings (4.8+): Bonus per delivery
- Consecutive deliveries: Route bonus
- Speed bonuses: Complete deliveries faster than estimated
- Customer satisfaction: Perfect rating streaks

**Tips**
- Patients can tip in app after delivery
- Tip shows after 1-2 hours of delivery
- Rating and tip available in earnings screen
- Popular delivery times get more tips

**Deductions**
- Cancellations after pickup: -$1
- Customer complaints: May result in deductions
- Refunds for failed deliveries: Net earnings reduced

## Route Planning

### Optimized Route Display

**Route Map**
- All today's deliveries shown on map
- Numbered stops in optimal order
- Distance between stops
- Estimated total drive time
- Click stop to navigate

**Route Navigation**
1. Click "Start Route" at beginning of day
2. All deliveries added to navigation
3. System suggests optimal order
4. You can reorder stops if needed
5. Navigate to next stop automatically

**Why Order Matters**
- Optimal route saves time and gas
- Faster completion = higher earnings
- Helps meet patient delivery windows
- Consider traffic patterns and pickup order

### Modifying Your Route

**Reorder Deliveries**
1. Open "Deliveries" tab
2. Long-press delivery to drag/reorder
3. Or use "Optimize Route" to let system reorder
4. Confirm new order

**Skip to Different Delivery**
1. If traffic prevents reaching next stop
2. Click "Navigate to different stop"
3. Select destination from list
4. Old stop stays in list (complete later)

**Take Delivery Break**
1. Click "Pause Shift" in navigation
2. Your location freezes on tracking
3. You're marked unavailable
4. Resume when ready (click "Resume Shift")

## GPS Tracking & Navigation

### Location Services

**Enable Location**
- GPS tracking is required for deliveries
- Pharmacy and patients can see your real-time location
- Location data deleted after delivery complete
- Your privacy respected when offline

**Accuracy**
- GPS accurate to within 5-10 meters
- Indoors may have reduced accuracy
- If stuck in building: Click "Mark Arrived" when at delivery address

**Background Tracking**
- Location tracked even if app in background
- Allows use of phone maps simultaneously
- Battery usage: ~15-20% per hour with GPS on
- Use car charger recommended

### Turn-by-Turn Navigation

**Built-in Navigation**
- App provides turn-by-turn directions
- Voice guidance (optional)
- Avoids toll roads if preferred
- Updates with traffic conditions

**Using External Maps**
- Prefer Google Maps or Apple Maps?
- Click "Navigate in Maps" to use external app
- Still tracked by MetaPharm for delivery completion
- Return to MetaPharm app when close to address

**When You Arrive**
1. Navigate to address
2. When within 50 meters, "Mark Arrived" available
3. Click "Mark Arrived" when at delivery location
4. Photo/scan verification required

## QR Code Scanning

### QR Code System

**Why QR Codes?**
- Verify you have correct medications
- Track delivery authenticity
- Prevent medication mix-ups
- Legal requirement for controlled substances

**QR Locations**
- On delivery package or label
- Multiple QR codes for large orders
- Some packages have one QR for entire delivery

### Scanning Process

**Before Pickup**
1. At pharmacy, pharmacy staff scan QR on your vehicle/uniform
2. Confirms you're authorized delivery person
3. Shows you've picked up delivery

**At Patient's Home**
1. Remove package from vehicle
2. Open MetaPharm app
3. Click "Scan QR Code"
4. Use phone camera to scan QR
5. System verifies package contents match patient order
6. Confirmation appears on screen
7. Proceed with delivery

**If Scan Fails**
1. Check QR code visibility (clean, not damaged)
2. Improve lighting
3. Try different angle
4. Contact pharmacy if damaged
5. Manual entry option available (last resort)

### Controlled Substance Handling

**Special Requirements**
- Some medications are controlled substances
- Require signature from patient
- Cannot be left unattended
- If patient unavailable, return to pharmacy
- Follow special handling notes in app

## Proof of Delivery

### Capture Proof of Delivery

**After Successful Scan**
1. You're at patient's door with package
2. System prompts for proof of delivery
3. Options available:
   - **Signature**: Patient signs on your phone
   - **Photo**: Picture of delivered package at door
   - **Selfie**: Photo with patient (optional)
   - **PIN**: Patient enters 4-digit PIN

**Which Proof Required?**
- Regular medications: Signature or photo
- Controlled substances: **Signature required**
- Recurring deliveries: Signature first time, then just photo
- Check app for specific delivery requirements

### Taking Photos

**Good Proof Photos**
- Show package at patient's door
- Clear view of address number/street name
- Package intact and readable
- Timestamp captured automatically
- Include patient if willing (privacy respected)

**Poor Photos**
- Blurry or dark images
- Can't see address or package
- Patient's face without consent
- These may be rejected, requiring redelivery

### Signature Capture

**Getting Patient Signature**
1. Patient signs on phone screen with finger
2. Signature appears in app
3. Confirm signature before submitting
4. If unsatisfactory, ask patient to sign again
5. Submit only when satisfied

**No Patient Home?**
- If no answer, don't leave package at door
- Click "Delivery Issue" → "No One Home"
- System creates delivery reattempt
- Same day or next day available
- Pharmacy contacts patient to reschedule

**Refusing Delivery**
- If patient refuses package
- Click "Delivery Issue" → "Patient Refused"
- Add reason if provided
- Take photo of package with refused note
- Return to pharmacy
- You still get base fare in most cases

## Special Handling

### Controlled Substances

**What Are They?**
- Narcotics and opioids
- Barbiturates
- Stimulants
- Other regulated medications

**Special Requirements**
- Signature from patient **required**
- Cannot be left at door
- If patient not home, return to pharmacy
- These deliveries pay 50% bonus

**Proper Handling**
- Keep secure in vehicle
- Don't leave unattended
- Verify patient identity if required
- Deliver during patient's stated availability

### Cold Chain Medications

**Temperature Control**
- Some medications must stay cool (2-8°C)
- Ice pack provided at pharmacy
- Insulated bag used for transport
- Keep in cooler while delivering
- Check ice at each delivery

**Your Responsibility**
- Ensure cold chain maintained
- Deliver quickly (time window specified)
- Don't leave in sun/heat
- Alert pharmacy if ice melting

### Hazardous Materials

**Biohazard Packages**
- Marked with biohazard symbol
- Follow handling instructions
- Don't open or damage package
- Clean hands after handling

**Allergenic Medications**
- Some patients have allergies
- App notes if patient allergic to peanuts, latex, etc.
- Avoid handling with bare hands if noted
- Use gloves provided

### Signature Requirements

**Always Required For:**
- Controlled substances
- High-value orders (>$500)
- Biohazardous materials
- As noted in special instructions
- Patient-specific requests

**Recommended For:**
- Large orders
- First-time patient
- Valuable medications
- Any concern about package

## Support & Help

### In-App Issues

**Technical Problems**
- GPS not working: Restart app, check permissions
- Scan failing: Check QR clarity, improve lighting
- Payment issues: Check payment method, contact support
- Notifications not working: Check app settings

**Delivery Issues**
- Wrong address: Confirm with patient and pharmacy
- Package damaged: Take photo, report in app
- Patient unavailable: Click delivery issue, reschedule
- Can't find address: Use Google Maps, call pharmacy

### Contact Support

**In-App Chat**
- Click "Help" → "Chat with Support"
- Available 6am-10pm daily
- Response within 5 minutes usually
- Type or voice message

**Email Support**
- support@metapharm.com
- Include:
  - Your name and driver ID
  - Delivery order number
  - Issue description
  - Screenshots if relevant
- Response within 4 hours

**Phone Support**
- +41 44 200 0000
- Available Mon-Fri, 9am-6pm
- Urgent issues: Always answered
- Queue times typical during peak hours

**Emergency Support**
- Traffic accident: Call 112, then app support
- Package theft: Call police (112), then app support
- Patient emergency: Call 112 for medical help
- Personal safety: Call police (112) first
