## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Receive Account Information**
   - Your facility administrator will create your account
   - You'll receive an email with login instructions

2. **First Login**
   - Go to MetaPharm Connect login page
   - Enter your email and temporary password
   - You'll be prompted to set a new password

3. **Two-Factor Authentication (MFA)**
   - MetaPharm requires MFA for all healthcare professionals
   - Set up authentication in your account settings
   - Use Google Authenticator or similar app

4. **Verify Professional Credentials**
   - Your nursing license and credentials will be verified
   - Process typically takes 24-48 hours
   - You'll receive confirmation email

### First Steps

1. **Update Your Profile**
   - Click Profile icon (top right) → "My Profile"
   - Add your photo and specialty
   - Set your work schedule and shift times
   - Enable notifications for your work hours

2. **Select Your Facility**
   - If you work at multiple facilities, select your primary one
   - You can switch facilities in Settings → Work Locations

3. **Review Assigned Patients**
   - Your facility admin will assign you a patient list
   - You can view all your patients in the Dashboard

## Medication Ordering

### Ordering Medications for Patients

**Step 1: Start Order**
1. Click "New Medication Order" from Dashboard
2. Select patient from list (or search by name)
3. Click "Continue"

**Step 2: Select Medications**
1. Search for medication by name or NDC code
2. For each medication:
   - Select dosage strength
   - Specify quantity needed
   - Set urgency (routine/urgent)
3. Add notes if needed (reason for order, special instructions)
4. Click "Add to Order"

**Step 3: Verify & Submit**
1. Review all items in order
2. Confirm patient allergies are considered
3. Add any additional notes for pharmacist
4. Click "Submit Order"
5. You'll receive confirmation with order number

**Step 4: Track Order Status**
- **Pending**: Awaiting pharmacist review
- **Approved**: Approved by pharmacist, ready to dispense
- **Dispensed**: Medications prepared, awaiting delivery
- **Delivered**: Delivered to patient
- **Cancelled**: Order cancelled by pharmacist (with notes)

### Ordering Restrictions & Approvals

**Controlled Substances**
- Some medications require additional approval
- Your facility may have ordering restrictions
- Pharmacist will contact you if approval needed
- Higher urgency orders reviewed faster

**Quantity Limits**
- Certain medications have maximum quantities per order
- Check quantity limits before ordering
- Pharmacist can approve larger quantities if medically necessary

**Insurance & Coverage**
- System checks insurance coverage
- If medication not covered, pharmacist will notify you
- Alternative medications suggested if available

### Medication Order History

**View Past Orders**
1. Click "My Orders" in Navigation
2. Filter by:
   - Patient name
   - Date range
   - Status (all, delivered, cancelled)
   - Facility
3. Click order to view details
4. Export as PDF if needed for records

### Adding Patient Notes

**Quick Note**
1. Open patient record
2. Scroll to Notes section
3. Click "Add Note"
4. Type your note (max 1000 characters)
5. Click "Save Note"

**Detailed Note**
1. Open patient record
2. Click "Add Clinical Note"
3. Select note type:
   - Medication administration
   - Patient reaction/observation
   - Communication with doctor
   - Care coordination
4. Add date and time
5. Type detailed note
6. Click "Save Note"

## Delivery Tracking

### Real-Time Delivery Tracking

**View Active Deliveries**
1. Click "Deliveries" in Navigation
2. View all active deliveries for your patients
3. Current status, location, and ETA displayed
4. Map shows real-time location

**Track Specific Delivery**
1. Click delivery in list or Dashboard
2. Full delivery details open in sidebar:
   - Patient name and address
   - What's being delivered
   - Current status
   - Delivery personnel name
   - Real-time GPS location
   - Estimated arrival time
   - Complete route history

**Receive Notifications**
- Automatic notifications for:
  - Delivery assigned
  - Delivery en route
  - Delivery approaching
  - Delivery complete
- Click notification to view delivery details

### Delivery Status Options

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| PENDING_ASSIGNMENT | Waiting for driver | None |
| ASSIGNED | Driver assigned, not yet picked up | Confirm patient is ready |
| IN_TRANSIT | On the way to patient | Notify patient of ETA |
| DELIVERED | Complete, signature captured | Confirm receipt in system |
| CANCELLED | Order cancelled | Contact pharmacist for details |

### Delivery Issues

**If Delivery is Late**
1. Check ETA in delivery tracking
2. If beyond ETA, click "Report Issue"
3. Select issue type:
   - Traffic/route issues
   - Patient not available
   - Delivery address issue
   - Other
4. Provide details and contact info
5. Pharmacist will follow up

**If Patient Refuses Delivery**
1. Click "Delivery Problem"
2. Select "Patient Refused"
3. Add reason if provided by patient
4. Pharmacist will contact patient

## Medication Administration

### Logging Medication Administration

**After Administering Medication**
1. Open patient record
2. Scroll to "Medication Administration"
3. Click "Log Administration"
4. Select medication from patient's current list
5. Enter:
   - Dosage administered
   - Route (oral, IV, injection, etc.)
   - Time administered
   - Site (if applicable)
   - Your initials/signature
6. Click "Save"

### Medication Schedule

**View Patient's Medication Schedule**
1. Open patient record
2. Click "Medication Schedule" tab
3. View:
   - All active medications
   - Dosages and frequencies
   - Scheduled times
   - Last administration time
   - Upcoming administrations

**Medication Reminders**
- System sends reminders for scheduled medications
- Customize reminder timing in Settings
- Set quiet hours (when reminders are muted)
- Receive reminders via:
  - In-app notifications
  - Email (optional)
  - SMS (optional)

### Reporting Adverse Reactions

**If Patient Has Medication Reaction**
1. Open patient record
2. Click "Report Adverse Reaction"
3. Fill form:
   - Medication that caused reaction
   - Type of reaction (rash, nausea, etc.)
   - Severity (mild, moderate, severe)
   - Time occurred
   - Actions taken (stopped medication, etc.)
   - Detailed description
4. Click "Submit Report"
5. Pharmacist and doctor notified automatically
6. Patient's allergy profile may be updated

**Allergy Documentation**
- Reactions are logged in patient's allergy section
- Color-coded alerts on patient record
- Future orders checked against allergies
- Alert notifications display prominently

## Communication

### Messaging with Pharmacist

**Send Message**
1. Click "Messages" in Navigation
2. Click "New Message" or search pharmacist
3. Type message
4. Attach files if needed (e.g., prescription images)
5. Click "Send"

**Message Types**
- Question about medication
- Clarification on prescription
- Report medication issue
- Request for approval
- General communication

**Response Times**
- During business hours: Within 30 minutes
- After hours: Urgent requests answered, routine next business day
- Emergency: Call facility manager or poison control

### Messaging with Doctors

**Request Clarification from Doctor**
1. Open patient record
2. Click "Message Doctor"
3. Type your question or note
4. Include relevant patient info
5. Click "Send"

**Doctor Availability**
- Check doctor's schedule in system
- Urgent issues: Call facility manager
- Non-urgent: Message reviewed next business day

### Secure Communication

**All messages are:**
- End-to-end encrypted
- Logged for compliance
- Searchable in message history
- Archived per healthcare regulations

**Never communicate:**
- Via personal email
- Via personal text/WhatsApp
- Without patient context

## Shift Handover

### Preparing Handover Notes

**Before Shift Ends**
1. Click "Handover Notes" in Navigation
2. For each patient you cared for:
   - Click "Add Handover Note"
   - Add current status
   - List medications administered
   - Note any reactions or issues
   - Mention pending deliveries
   - Flag any concerns for next shift
3. Include:
   - Patient's overall condition
   - Any medication changes needed
   - Pending doctor communications
   - Important observations
4. Click "Save & Submit"

### Receiving Handover

**Start of Shift**
1. Click "Handover Notes" in Navigation
2. View notes from previous shift
3. Review all patients assigned to you
4. Check for:
   - Patient status changes
   - New medications
   - Pending deliveries
   - Important alerts
5. Ask previous nurse clarifying questions if needed
6. Mark as "Reviewed" to confirm understanding

## FAQ & Support

### Common Questions

**Q: Can I order medications for patients not in my assignment?**
A: No, you can only order for your assigned patients. Contact your facility admin to add patients to your list.

**Q: How quickly are medication orders typically processed?**
A: Most orders within 2 hours during business hours. Urgent orders prioritized. Check status in your orders list.

**Q: What if I need to modify an order after submitting?**
A: If order not yet approved, click "Edit" on order. If approved, contact pharmacist - they may be able to adjust.

**Q: Can I see previous deliveries for tracking historical data?**
A: Yes, click "Deliveries" → "Delivery History" to view all past deliveries for your patients.

**Q: How do I handle a medication that patient refuses?**
A: Document refusal in medication administration notes with reason. Message pharmacist. Don't force medication.

**Q: What's the difference between patient refusal and adverse reaction?**
A: Refusal = patient chooses not to take medication. Adverse reaction = patient has negative physical response to medication.

**Q: Can I delete or modify notes I've entered?**
A: You can edit notes for 24 hours. After that, contact facility admin. All changes are audited.

**Q: How do I handle narcotic/controlled substance orders?**
A: Same process, but requires facility-level approval. Your facility admin sets these restrictions.

**Q: What if the delivery address in the system is wrong?**
A: Contact patient directly to confirm correct address. Contact pharmacist before delivery to update address if needed.
