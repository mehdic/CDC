## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Download the App** or visit web.metapharm.com
2. **Sign Up**:
   - Create account with email
   - Verify email address
   - Set password (minimum 12 characters)
3. **Complete Profile**:
   - Full name
   - Date of birth
   - Phone number
   - Address
   - Insurance information (optional)
4. **Enable 2FA** (recommended)
   - Go to Settings → Security
   - Enable two-factor authentication
   - Save backup codes

### First Steps

1. **Add Healthcare Providers**:
   - Go to Profile → Healthcare Providers
   - Search by name or email
   - Send connection request
   - Doctor/Pharmacist approves

2. **Set Delivery Address**:
   - Profile → Addresses
   - Add primary delivery address
   - Add backup addresses (optional)

3. **Enable Notifications**:
   - Settings → Notifications
   - Turn on prescription and delivery alerts
   - Set quiet hours if needed

## Managing Prescriptions

### View Your Prescriptions

1. **Click** "Prescriptions" tab
2. **See** Active, completed, and expired prescriptions
3. **Filter** by date, doctor, or status

**Prescription Details**:
- Drug name and strength
- Dosage instructions
- Frequency (2x daily, etc.)
- Duration
- Doctor who prescribed
- Pharmacy validating
- Status (pending, validated, dispensed, expired)

### Prescription Status Explained

**PENDING_VALIDATION**:
- Doctor created prescription
- Pharmacist hasn't reviewed yet
- You can't order until validated

**VALIDATED**:
- Pharmacist checked for interactions
- Ready to order
- Valid for 6 months (depending on drug regulations)

**DISPENSED**:
- Medication has been delivered
- Prescription marked as used

**EXPIRED**:
- Prescription no longer valid
- Ask doctor to renew

### Request Prescription Renewal

1. **Go to** Prescriptions
2. **Click** prescription to renew
3. **Click** "Request Renewal"
4. **Add** message (optional)
5. **Click** "Send Request"
6. **Doctor** reviews and creates new prescription

### Prescription Reminders

**Set Reminders**:
1. **Click** prescription
2. **Click** "Set Reminder"
3. **Choose** frequency:
   - Daily
   - Weekly
   - Custom days
4. **Set Time**: When to take medication
5. **Click** "Save"

**Notification**: Get alert at reminder time

## Ordering Medications

### How to Order

**Option 1: Order from Prescription**:
1. **Go to** Prescriptions
2. **Click** validated prescription
3. **Click** "Order Medication"
4. **Review** pharmacy
5. **Click** "Confirm Order"

**Option 2: Order OTC/Parapharmacy**:
1. **Click** "Shop" or "Browse Catalog"
2. **Search** for product
3. **Click** product
4. **Choose** quantity
5. **Click** "Add to Cart"
6. **Click** "Checkout"
7. **Confirm** order

**Option 3: Quick Reorder**:
1. **Click** "Recent Orders"
2. **Click** "Reorder" on previous order
3. **Confirm** details
4. **Click** "Place Order"

### Order Confirmation

After placing order, you'll see:

**Order Details**:
- Order number
- Items and quantities
- Total price
- Delivery address
- Estimated delivery date

**Next Steps**:
- Pharmacist validates (if prescription)
- Medication prepared
- Delivery assigned
- You receive SMS/push notification with tracking link

## Teleconsultation

### Starting a Consultation

**Option 1: From Dashboard**:
1. **Click** "Schedule Consultation"
2. **Choose** doctor or therapist
3. **Select** date and time
4. **Add** reason (optional)
5. **Click** "Request Consultation"
6. **Provider** confirms

**Option 2: Direct Message**:
1. **Click** Messages
2. **Select** provider
3. **Click** Video icon
4. **Provider** answers call

### During Consultation

**Call Features**:
- **Video** - See your doctor
- **Audio** - Clear sound
- **Chat** - Text messages during call
- **Screen Share** - Doctor can share information
- **Recording** - With permission for medical records

**After Consultation**:
- Receipt of consultation saved
- Prescription issued if needed
- Notes added to medical record
- Recording available (if recorded)

### Scheduling Tips

- **Book in advance** - Doctors fill up quickly
- **Be specific** - Describe your issue clearly
- **Prepare** - Have insurance card, medications list ready
- **Test** - Check camera/microphone before call

## Messaging

### Send Message to Healthcare Provider

1. **Click** Messages icon
2. **Click** "New Message"
3. **Select** doctor, pharmacist, or nurse
4. **Type** message
5. **Click** "Send"

**Message Features**:
- **Attach Files** - Share documents or photos
- **Read Receipts** - See when provider reads message
- **Message Search** - Find past conversations

### Message Response Times

- **Pharmacist**: Usually responds within 2 hours
- **Doctor**: Usually responds within 4 hours
- **Urgent**: Call pharmacy or use 911 for emergencies

### Message Etiquette

**Do**:
- Be clear and concise
- Describe symptoms in detail
- Ask specific questions

**Don't**:
- Send medical emergencies via message
- Share sensitive data in messages
- Use for urgent time-sensitive issues

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! All data is encrypted end-to-end. We comply with HIPAA and GDPR standards.

**Q: How much do consultations cost?**
A: Depends on provider and type. Most insurance covers consultations. Check before booking.

**Q: Can I see my prescription history?**
A: Yes! Go to Prescriptions → All, or Health Records → Prescriptions.

**Q: How do I request a prescription?**
A: Go to Prescriptions → "Request New" and describe what you need.

### Ordering & Delivery

**Q: How fast can medication be delivered?**
A: Standard delivery is 1-3 days. Express delivery (2 hours) available in cities.

**Q: Can I cancel an order?**
A: Yes, if order hasn't been prepared yet. Go to Orders and click Cancel.

**Q: What if I'm not home for delivery?**
A: You can request "leave at door" in delivery instructions.

**Q: Do you deliver on weekends?**
A: Yes, in most areas. Check delivery options when ordering.

### Prescriptions

**Q: Why is my prescription showing "PENDING_VALIDATION"?**
A: Pharmacist hasn't reviewed it yet. This usually takes 1-4 hours.

**Q: Can I get multiple months of medication at once?**
A: Some medications yes, some no. Depends on regulations. Check with pharmacist.

**Q: What if I have an allergic reaction to medication?**
A: STOP taking it and call your doctor or emergency services immediately.

**Q: How long are prescriptions valid?**
A: Usually 6 months for regular drugs, 1 month for controlled substances.

### Account & Security

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Settings → Security → Change Password.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, one email = one account. If you need multiple, use different emails.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. This is permanent!

**Q: Is two-factor authentication required?**
A: No, but highly recommended for security.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The app keeps crashing.**
A: Try: 1) Force close app, 2) Clear cache, 3) Reinstall app, 4) Contact support.

**Q: I can't log in.**
A: Try: 1) Reset password, 2) Clear browser cache, 3) Try different browser, 4) Contact support.

**Q: Videos won't play in consultations.**
A: Check: 1) Camera/mic permissions granted, 2) Internet connection stable, 3) Browser updated.
