## Getting Started

### Account Setup

**Registration**:
1. **Sign Up** with professional email
2. **License Verification** (24-48 hours):
   - Enter medical license number
   - Provide proof of credentials
   - Swiss cantonal registry validation
   - Approval before full access
3. **Create Profile**:
   - Full name
   - Specialty
   - Hospital/Clinic affiliation
   - Phone number
4. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** (mandatory)
   - Use authenticator app or SMS
   - Save backup codes

### Your Responsibilities

As a doctor on MetaPharm Connect, you:
- ✅ Create and manage prescriptions
- ✅ Review patient health records
- ✅ Conduct teleconsultations

- ✅ Maintain patient privacy and compliance
- ✅ Document all clinical decisions

## Prescribing Medications

### Create New Prescription

**Step-by-Step**:

1. **Click** "Prescriptions" → "New Prescription"
2. **Select Patient**:
   - Search by name or email
   - Confirm patient identity
3. **Choose Drug**:
   - Search by name
   - See drug database with interactions
   - View available strengths
4. **Set Dosage**:
   - Strength (e.g., 500mg)
   - Frequency (e.g., 2x daily)
   - Duration (e.g., 7 days)
   - Quantity (number of doses)
5. **Add Special Instructions**:
   - Take with food
   - Avoid alcohol
   - Side effect warnings
   - What to do if dose missed
6. **Confirm Patient Allergies**:
   - System shows all known allergies
   - Make sure no interactions
7. **Confirm Medication Interactions**:
   - System checks against current medications
   - Red alerts = serious interactions
   - Yellow alerts = caution
8. **Review & Submit**:
   - Double-check all details
   - Verify patient information
   - Click "Write Prescription"

### Prescribing Workflow

**Flow**:
```
You write prescription
        ↓
Pharmacy receives & validates
        ↓
Check for interactions
        ↓
Approve or request changes
        ↓
Patient orders medication
        ↓
Pharmacy prepares & ships
        ↓
Patient receives medication
```

### Renewing Prescriptions

**Patient Requests Renewal**:
1. **Receive** patient request in Messages
2. **Click** the prescription link
3. **Review** original prescription
4. **Create New Prescription**:
   - Same drug, strength, frequency
   - New duration
   - Same or modified instructions
5. **Submit** new prescription

**Automatic Refills**:
1. **During prescription creation**, check "Enable auto refill"
2. **Set** refill frequency (e.g., every 30 days)
3. **Patient** automatically gets refills
4. **You receive** notification of each refill
5. **Cancel** auto-refill anytime

### Prescription Status

**Track Your Prescriptions**:
1. **Click** "Prescriptions"
2. **See** Status of each:
   - **PENDING_VALIDATION** - Pharmacist reviewing
   - **VALIDATED** - Ready for patient to order
   - **DISPENSED** - Patient picked up/received
   - **EXPIRED** - No longer valid

**When Pharmacist Requests Changes**:
1. **Receive** notification
2. **Review** pharmacist's concern
3. **Modify prescription**:
   - Change drug to alternative
   - Adjust dosage
   - Add special instructions
4. **Re-submit** prescription
5. **Pharmacist validates** modified version

### Common Prescribing Scenarios

**Chronic Medication**:
- Patient with diabetes needing daily insulin
- Enable auto-refill every 30 days
- Patient can always reorder through app

**Short Course Antibiotic**:
- 7-day course for infection
- No auto-refill
- Patient orders single course

**As-Needed Medication**:
- PRN pain medication
- Patient orders when needed
- Write with "As needed, max 3x daily"

## Communication

### Message Patient

**Send Message**:
1. **Click** patient name
2. **Click** "Message"
3. **Type** message
4. **Click** "Send"

**Message Examples**:
- "Your lab results are ready. All normal!"
- "I'd like to see you for a follow-up next week"
- "Please schedule the appointment I recommended"
- "Did you start the medication? Any side effects?"

### Message Response Times

- **Routine**: Within 24 hours
- **Urgent**: Within 2 hours
- **Emergency**: Call 911

### Message Best Practices

**Do**:
- Be clear and specific
- Offer options for next steps
- Schedule follow-ups
- Acknowledge patient concerns

**Don't**:
- Use for emergencies (call instead)
- Share sensitive data in messages
- Practice medicine via chat
- Make major decisions without consulting

### Communicate with Pharmacist

**When to Contact Pharmacist**:
- Patient has drug interaction concern
- Need recommendation on alternative drug
- Question about drug availability
- Coordination of care

**How to Contact**:
1. **From prescription**, click "Contact Pharmacist"
2. **Enter** your message
3. **Click** "Send"
4. **Pharmacist** responds within hours

## Best Practices

### Prescription Safety

1. **Always verify** patient identity
2. **Check allergies** before prescribing
3. **Review** current medications
4. **Consider** drug interactions
5. **Double-check** dosage calculations
6. **Confirm** patient can afford medication
7. **Follow** Swiss medical guidelines

### Consultation Etiquette

1. **Start on time** - Respect patient's schedule
2. **Listen carefully** - Understand symptoms
3. **Explain clearly** - Patient understands treatment
4. **Document fully** - Complete notes for record
5. **Follow-up** - Schedule next appointment

### Patient Communication

1. **Respond promptly** - Patients appreciate quick responses
2. **Be professional** - Maintain doctor-patient relationship
3. **Use plain language** - Avoid medical jargon when possible
4. **Show empathy** - Healthcare is personal
5. **Respect privacy** - Use secure channels

### Record Keeping

1. **Document all** consultations and prescriptions
2. **Include** clinical reasoning for decisions
3. **Note** patient preferences and concerns
4. **Update** health records regularly
5. **Maintain** confidentiality

## FAQs

### Prescribing Questions

**Q: Can I prescribe controlled substances?**
A: Only if you have authorization. Your profile shows which drugs you can prescribe.

**Q: What if pharmacist rejects my prescription?**
A: They'll contact you with concerns. You can modify and resubmit, or discuss alternatives.

**Q: How long are prescriptions valid?**
A: Usually 6 months for regular drugs, 1 month for controlled substances (depends on regulations).

**Q: Can patient get multiple months of medication?**
A: Some drugs yes, some no. Check with pharmacist about quantity limits.

**Q: What if patient has no insurance?**
A: Patient still receives medication. They pay out-of-pocket or can apply for subsidies.

### Teleconsultation Questions

**Q: What if patient doesn't have camera?**
A: Audio-only consultation still works. Recommend camera for next time.

**Q: Can I record consultations?**
A: Yes, but MUST get patient written consent first.

**Q: What if there's technical issue?**
A: Call patient on phone instead. Note technical issue in record.

**Q: How long should consultations be?**
A: 15-30 minutes typically. Complex cases may take longer.

### Patient Record Questions

**Q: What information must I document?**
A: Chief complaint, history, exam findings, assessment, plan, and any prescriptions written.

**Q: Can I access patient records from other doctors?**
A: Only if patient has authorized sharing. They control which records are visible.

**Q: How long are records kept?**
A: Minimum 5 years from last visit (Swiss law). We keep longer for continuity of care.

**Q: What if patient requests to delete records?**
A: Inform support. Deletion may not be allowed if required for legal/compliance reasons.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I update my profile?**
A: Click Profile icon → "My Profile" → Edit information → Save changes.

**Q: Can I have colleagues access my patient list?**
A: Not directly. Each doctor maintains their own patient list.

**Q: What if I lose access (license suspended)?**
A: Contact support immediately. We'll investigate and may suspend access.

**Q: How often do I need to re-verify credentials?**
A: Swiss license verification is valid for 5 years or until you change practice location.
